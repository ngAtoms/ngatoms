Drawer

A panel that slides in from an edge of the screen, over a backdrop. Built on the native <dialog> element, so focus trapping and Escape come for free.

ngAtoms v1.0+ ngatoms add drawer Alpha Source

Edit profile

Changes are saved to your account.

Put a form, filters, or details here. The body scrolls when the content is taller than the screen.

Usage

Add the drawer to your project and import it wherever you need it.

$ ngatoms add drawer

Then import into your component:

Sides

side picks the edge the drawer slides from. right is the default.

side="{{ side() }}"

Press Escape or click the backdrop to close.

Sizes

size sets the width of a left or right drawer, or the height of a top or bottom one. full covers the whole screen.

size="{{ size() }}"

Press Escape or click the backdrop to close.

Dismissal

Clicking the backdrop closes the drawer. Set [dismissible]="false" to require an explicit action; Escape still closes it, as it does for any native dialog.
